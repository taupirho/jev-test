PUBLIC INFORMATION: COMMUNITY GARDEN GUIDE

A community garden is a shared space for growing flowers, herbs, and
vegetables. Choose a sunny location with access to water and healthy soil.

Getting started
1. Remove weeds and loosen the soil.
2. Mix finished compost into the top layer.
3. Plant seeds at the depth printed on the packet.
4. Water gently and check the soil regularly.
5. Label each row with the name of the plant.

Seasonal activities
In spring, prepare beds and sow suitable seeds.
In summer, water early and harvest ripe produce.
In autumn, collect fallen leaves for compost.
In winter, clean tools and plan the next growing season.

Shared garden etiquette
Keep paths clear, return borrowed tools, and place plant waste in the
compost area. Leave flowers for pollinators and avoid disturbing wildlife.

This general guide is intended for public distribution.
